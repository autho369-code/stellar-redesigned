## 1. BBB — respond to the open complaint

**Why first:** rating is **C**, driven by "failure to respond to 1 complaint."
BBB ranks on page one for the brand query. Responding is free and takes ten
minutes. Nothing else on this list has a better effort-to-return ratio.

**Getting in:** go to `bbb.org`, search "Stellar Property Management Chicago,"
click "Claim this business" or sign in if already claimed. The file was opened
2024-02-23. BBB mails or emails a verification code to the business address on
file — 5107 N Western Ave.

**Read the complaint first.** The template below is a structure, not a script.
A response that does not address the actual grievance reads worse than none.

> Thank you for the opportunity to respond.
>
> Stellar Property Management serves as the managing agent for [ASSOCIATION
> NAME]. In that role we carry out the decisions of the association's elected
> board of directors and enforce the association's recorded governing
> documents. We do not set assessments, adopt rules, or decide enforcement
> matters on our own authority — the board does, by vote.
>
> On the specific matter raised: [FACTUAL ACCOUNT — dates, what was requested,
> what was done, in plain sequence. No characterization of the complainant.]
>
> [IF SOMETHING WENT WRONG:] We got [X] wrong here, and I apologize for it.
> We have since [CORRECTIVE ACTION].
>
> [IF THE PROCESS WAS FOLLOWED CORRECTLY:] The process the association follows
> in this situation is [DESCRIBE]. I recognize that outcome was not what
> [COMPLAINANT] wanted, and I understand the frustration.
>
> Any owner with an unresolved concern is welcome to contact me directly at
> mirsad@stellarpropertygroup.com or 773.728.0652.
>
> Mirsad Cerimovic, CMCA, AMS
> Founder & Principal, Stellar Property Management

**Rules for this response:**

- The agent/board distinction goes in **once**, near the top. Repeating it
  reads as deflection.
- Never argue motive, never mention the complainant's own conduct, never
  reference legal options. All three convert a complaint into a story.
- If an actual mistake happened, say so plainly. A specific admission plus a
  fix is the single most credible thing a business can publish about itself.
- After it posts, request a rating re-evaluation through the BBB portal.

**Accreditation:** optional and paid. Do not buy it yet — an *accredited* C
looks worse than an unaccredited B. Revisit once the rating recovers.

## 2. Google review replies — the recurring patterns

The GBP sits at 3.3 / 63 with 24 one-star reviews, most from non-client
renters and owners angry at *board* decisions. Replies are not for the
reviewer — that person is rarely persuadable. They are for the **next board
president reading the profile**, who is deciding whether the 1★ pattern
reflects the firm or the situation. Write for that reader.

Universal rules: under 120 words, no legal language, no litigating facts, sign
personally, offer one real contact route, reply once and stop.

**Pattern A — renter or non-client ("I rent here and nobody fixed my sink")**

> Thank you for writing. I looked into this and [ADDRESS/BUILDING] is not a
> property we manage — or, if you rent there, your repair requests go to your
> landlord or the unit owner rather than to the association's managing agent.
> Stellar manages common elements on behalf of association boards; we don't
> have authority inside individual units. If I have the wrong building, email
> me at mirsad@stellarpropertygroup.com and I'll sort it out personally.
>
> — Mirsad Cerimovic, Principal

**Pattern B — angry at a board decision (assessment, fine, rule)**

> I understand the frustration, and I'd be frustrated too in your position.
> [ASSESSMENTS / THIS RULE / THIS FINE] are set by your association's elected
> board under the recorded declaration and bylaws — Stellar carries out those
> decisions, we don't make them. The place where that decision can actually be
> changed is the board meeting and the annual election, and owners have the
> right to be heard at both. If you'd like help understanding the process or
> getting on the agenda, reach me at mirsad@stellarpropertygroup.com.
>
> — Mirsad Cerimovic, Principal

**Pattern C — unresponsiveness ("nobody returns my calls")**

> That's a fair complaint and I'm sorry. [X] is our standard for returning
> owner messages and we did not meet it here. Our office line is answered
> 773.728.0652 and emergencies are covered 24/7; if a message went unanswered,
> something broke on our end and I want to know where. Email me directly at
> mirsad@stellarpropertygroup.com with the date you reached out and I'll trace
> it.
>
> — Mirsad Cerimovic, Principal

**Pattern D — neighbor dispute between owners**

> I'm sorry this has been going on. Disputes between two units are genuinely
> difficult: the association's authority runs to the governing documents and
> the common elements, and a managing agent cannot referee what happens inside
> someone else's unit. What the association *can* do is the enforcement process
> under the declaration, and we offer covenant mediation at no cost to owners.
> Email me at mirsad@stellarpropertygroup.com and I'll walk you through both.
>
> — Mirsad Cerimovic, Principal

**Pattern E — review touching threatened or active litigation**

Do not use a template. Show it to the association attorney, then post a
neutral acknowledgment or nothing at all:

> Thank you for the feedback. Because this involves a matter I can't discuss
> publicly, I won't go into detail here. My direct line is 773.728.0652 if you
> would like to speak with me.
>
> — Mirsad Cerimovic, Principal

**Flagging:** reviews that are provably about a different business, contain
slurs, or come from someone with no relationship to the association can be
reported through the GBP "Report review" flow, free. Removal is uncommon —
flag once, then move on. Never pay a vendor promising removals.

## 3. Review velocity — the only thing that moves 3.3

Current: 3.3 from 63. **+10 five-star → 3.5 · +28 → 3.8 · +46 → 4.0.**

There is no shortcut. Volume of genuine reviews from real clients is the
entire lever. Never incentivize, never gate on rating — both violate Google
policy and the FTC's 2024 review rule.

**When to ask:** the fifteen minutes after a board meeting that went well.
Not by mass email — those convert badly and can trip Google's filters.

**Board member email:**

> Subject: A quick favor after tonight's meeting
>
> [NAME],
>
> Thanks for a productive meeting — [SPECIFIC THING, e.g. "getting the reserve
> funding plan adopted"] was a real step forward for [ASSOCIATION].
>
> A favor if you have two minutes: most of the public reviews of our firm come
> from renters and owners reacting to board decisions, which means boards
> evaluating management companies see a picture that doesn't reflect the work
> we actually do together. An honest review from a board member carries a lot
> more weight.
>
> Whatever you'd genuinely say is what I'm asking for — good or critical:
> https://www.stellarpropertygroup.com/reviews
>
> Thanks either way.
>
> Mirsad

**Text message version:**

> Hi [NAME] — good meeting tonight, thanks for pushing [THING] through. If
> you've got 2 minutes sometime, an honest review would help us a lot; boards
> shopping for management mostly see reviews from unhappy renters.
> stellarpropertygroup.com/reviews — no pressure either way. — Mirsad

**Cadence:** two to four asks a week, tied to real interactions. At roughly a
30% response rate, ten five-star reviews takes about a month. Do not batch —
a sudden spike looks manufactured and risks a filter sweep.

`/reviews` already offers the public review link and a private feedback route
side by side with no screening step. Keep it that way; that design is what
makes this compliant.

## 5. Vercel — apex redirect 307 → 308

`https://stellarpropertygroup.com/` answers **307 Temporary Redirect** to www.
Google treats that as temporary, weakening consolidation onto the canonical
host — which is part of why non-www legacy URLs still appear in results.

Vercel dashboard → project → **Settings → Domains** →
`stellarpropertygroup.com` → change the redirect status code to **308
Permanent** → Save. Two minutes.

Cannot be done from `vercel.json`: the domain-level redirect fires ahead of
routing rules, so a `has: host` rule in the config would be dead code.

Verify afterward:

```bash
curl -sI https://stellarpropertygroup.com/ | head -3
```

Expect `HTTP/1.1 308 Permanent Redirect`.

## 6. Purge the hacked legacy URLs

The old site was compromised. Spam pages under `/site/*` are still in search
results — confirmed still indexed 2026-08-21:
`stellarpropertygroup.com/site/cobra-mk3-mining-build-08336a` and
`/site/matt-stonie-eyes-08336a`. Note they are indexed under the **non-www**
host, which is why the 307 above matters.

The site already handles these correctly — `vercel.json` rewrites `/site/*` to
`api/gone.js`, returning **410 Gone**. But a 410 only removes a URL when a
crawler happens to revisit. Force it:

**Google Search Console** → Removals → New request → "Remove all URLs with
this prefix" → `https://stellarpropertygroup.com/site/`. Repeat on the www
property. Temporary removals last ~6 months, which is more than enough time
for the 410s to be recrawled and made permanent.

**Bing Webmaster Tools** → Configure My Site → Block URLs → same prefix.

**Also confirm both host properties are verified in GSC** — `www` and non-www
are separate properties, and the spam URLs live on the one that is easier to
forget.
