# Stellar Property Group: blog internal-linking and silo fix plan
Prepared Oct 28, 2026 from a crawl of https://www.stellarpropertygroup.com (sitemap.xml lists 68 blog posts; every post and every target URL below returned HTTP 200 on Oct 28, 2026). Link data comes from each post's `<article>` body only. Nav, header and footer were excluded.

## 1. Context for the editor (Claude)
- **Site:** Stellar Property Group / Stellar Property Management, 5107 N Western Ave 1S, Chicago. It serves condominium, HOA and townhome associations in Chicago and the North Shore. The readers are board members and owners.
- **Structure:** a reverse silo. Blog posts are the supporting pages, and they push authority **up** to money pages: services, pricing, and the area hubs.
- **Rules to apply to every post:**
  1. Link up to **one** primary money page (listed per post below) with natural, descriptive anchor text in body copy. Put the link where the topic is discussed, not only in the CTA.
  2. Add at most **one** area page (`/property-management-chicago`, `/property-management-north-shore`, or a specific neighborhood page). Use it only where the post genuinely concerns that area.
  3. Add 1–2 **sibling** links to posts in the same silo.
  4. Posts in an overlap cluster (section 4) must link to that cluster's **pillar** post.
  5. Remove the extra money-page links from the body. Posts currently link to as many as 9 service/area pages, which dilutes the silo.
  6. Keep `/contact` (CTA) and `/about/mirsad-cerimovic` (author byline) as they are.
  7. Do **not** change the nav, header, footer, or the money pages' own content, except as noted in section 6.
  8. Change only links and short connecting sentences. Do not rewrite the substance of the posts.
